# Legal issue categories

Based on the official LegalBench / Learned Hands task descriptions. A post can involve several categories at once; mark every category that applies.

**benefits**: Public benefits and social services from the government, for food, disability, old age, housing, medical help, unemployment, child care or other needs: applying, eligibility, denials, overpayments. Not private insurance (that is consumer or health).

**business**: Issues faced by people who run a small business or nonprofit: incorporation, licenses, taxes, regulations, business contracts, and disasters or bankruptcy of the business. A person only buying from a business is consumer, not business.

**consumer**: Money problems of individuals: debts and debt collection, credit, loans, insurance, taxes, consumer goods and contracts, scams, and small claims about the quality of a service.

**courts**: The logistics of dealing with a lawyer or the court system: procedure and rules, how to file or answer a lawsuit, court dates, hiring or paying a lawyer, representing yourself. Not courts just because a legal problem exists or might go to court; the post must ask about or describe dealing with a court or lawyer.

**crime**: The criminal system: being charged with a crime, criminal trials, prison, criminal records, or being the victim of a crime and dealing with the police about it.

**divorce**: Filing for divorce, separation or annulment, spousal support, splitting money and property, and the divorce court process. Divorce posts are also family.

**domestic_violence**: Domestic violence and abuse: protective or restraining orders, enforcing them, understanding or reporting abuse, and getting resources or status because of abuse. These posts are usually also family.

**education**: School and education: accommodations for special needs, discrimination, discipline, student debt, and other issues with schools or colleges.

**employment**: Working at a job: discrimination and harassment at work, workers' compensation, workers' rights, unions, getting paid, pensions, being fired.

**estates**: Planning for death or incapacity and settling what is left: wills, inheritance and probate, powers of attorney, advance directives, trusts, guardianship and conservatorship of adults.

**family**: Issues within a family: divorce, adoption, name change, guardianship of children, domestic violence, child custody and support, paternity, and other disputes between relatives.

**health**: Accessing health services, paying for medical care, public benefits for health care, rights in medical settings, and other health-related issues.

**housing**: Paying rent or a mortgage, landlord-tenant issues, housing subsidies and public housing, eviction, and other problems with an apartment, mobile home or house.

**immigration**: Visas, asylum, green cards, citizenship, migrant work and benefits, and other issues of people who are not full US citizens.

**torts**: Problems one person has with another person or animal that cause harm: car accidents, dog bites, injuries, property damage, bullying or harassment, defamation, and neighbors treating each other badly.

**traffic**: The traffic system: traffic and parking tickets, fees, driver's licenses, car accidents and injuries, and problems with a car's quality, repairs, purchase or related contracts.
